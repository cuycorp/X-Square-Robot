From a typed command to motion

The language model only proposes. Fixed code decides whether a move is allowed, and you confirm every plan.

  1. 1 · YOU

    Type a command

    left arm up 1 cm or pick the cube in front of you. Directions are from the robot's point of view.

  2. 2 · NEBIUS

    Turn words into a move

    Returns JSON: arm, translation, rotation, gripper; or a pick target; or a clarifying question.

  3. 3 · PICKS ONLY

    Find the object in 3D

    Vision-model box (0–1000 scale) → depth camera → position in base_link, with the 7.3 cm camera correction.

  4. 4 · SAFETY PLANNER

    Check every step

    IK from the robot's own URDF. Step ≤ 3 cm / 15°, 5° joint-limit margin, workspace and reach, table clearance and arm separation at 6 points along the path.

  5. 5 · YOU

    Confirm

    Plan must be under 60 s old and the arm must not have moved more than 1° since planning.

  6. 6 · SDK

    Send gently

    Robot in SDK mode, joint-position control, targets in ≤ 0.5° pieces at 50 Hz. Aborts if the mode switch alone moves the arm.

  7. 7 · WATCH

    Confirm arrival

    Within 4 mm in 4 s, or stop. A force jump over 15 N stops and holds. The red E-stop always works.
